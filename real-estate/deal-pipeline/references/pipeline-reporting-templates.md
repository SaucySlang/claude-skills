# Pipeline Reporting Templates

## Weekly Pipeline Review

Run every Monday morning. Takes 15-20 minutes. Share with team or accountability partner by EOD Monday.

### Weekly Agenda
1. **Pipeline snapshot** (5 min) — stage counts vs last week, new leads added, deals closed
2. **Stale deal review** (5 min) — identify and assign actions for every stale deal
3. **Source performance** (3 min) — which sources are producing, any to cut or scale?
4. **Bottleneck focus** (5 min) — what is the one thing killing conversion this week?
5. **Next week targets** (2 min) — commit to specific numbers (calls, appointments, offers)

## KPIs Every Wholesaler Should Track

| KPI | Target | Frequency |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| New leads added | 20-50/week | Weekly |
| Contact rate | >60% of new leads | Weekly |
| Appointment rate | 10-20% of contacted | Weekly |
| Offer rate | >50% of appointments | Weekly |
| Contract rate | 30-50% of offers | Weekly |
| Close rate | >80% of contracts | Weekly |
| Avg days lead-to-contract | ≤21 days | Monthly |
| Avg days contract-to-close | ≤21 days | Monthly |
| Deals per month | ≥3 | Monthly |
| Average fee | ≥$12,000 | Monthly |

---

## Red Flag Indicators

These signals mean your pipeline is broken — fix before adding more leads:

- **Contact rate <40%:** Your skip trace data is bad or outreach volume is too low
- **Appointment rate <5%:** Your pitch/script needs work, or your list is wrong
- **Offer rate <30% of appointments:** You are not qualifying well enough before driving out
- **Stale deals >25% of active pipeline:** Deal stage hygiene is broken; add a TC or daily review
- **Contract-to-close fall-through >20%:** Buyer vetting or contract language needs fixing
- **Single source >60% of leads:** You are dangerously concentrated — diversify immediately
