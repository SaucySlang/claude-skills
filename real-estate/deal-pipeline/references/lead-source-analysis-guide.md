# Lead Source Analysis Guide

## Why Source Tracking Matters

Every dollar you spend on marketing should be measurable. The difference between wholesalers who scale and those who plateau is almost always found in their lead source data. If you don't know your cost per deal by source, you're guessing at your marketing budget.

---

## Setting Up Source Tracking

**Tag every lead at entry** with its exact source. Use consistent naming (no variations like "direct mail" vs "DM" vs "mailers"). Recommended source codes:

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `direct_mail` | Mailers sent to lists (absentee, probate, pre-foreclosure) |
| `cold_call` | Outbound calls from list |
| `probate` | Probate court filings research |
| `driving_for_dollars` | DFD route, physical scouting |
| `referral` | Agent, attorney, or investor referral |
| `facebook_ads` | Paid Facebook/Instagram campaigns |
| `google_ads` | Paid Google search campaigns |
| `bandit_signs` | Physical signage |
| `seo_website` | Organic website inbound |
| `ppc_website` | Paid search website inbound |
| `wholesaler_jv` | JV deal from another wholesaler |
| `auction` | Courthouse or online auction |

## Key Metrics by Source

### Cost Per Lead (CPL)
`Total marketing spend ÷ total leads generated`

Benchmark ranges (vary widely by market):
- Direct mail: $30-$80/lead
- Cold calling: $5-$20/lead (labor cost)
- Facebook ads: $20-$60/lead
- Referrals: $0 (or referral fee on close)
- Probate research: $10-$30/lead (time cost)

### Conversion Rate
`Closed deals ÷ total leads × 100`

Industry benchmarks:
- Overall: 1-3% of all leads to closed deal
- Cold calling: 0.5-1.5%
- Direct mail: 0.5-2%
- Referrals: 10-25%
- Inbound website: 3-8%

### Cost Per Deal (CPD)
`Total source spend ÷ deals closed from that source`

Target: CPD should be <15% of your average assignment fee.

### ROI
`(Revenue - Cost) ÷ Cost × 100`

Minimum target: 200% ROI (3x return). Scale sources at 300%+ ROI.

---

## Decision Framework

| ROI | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 300%+ | **Scale** — increase budget 25-50% next period |
| 100-299% | **Maintain** — keep steady, optimize creative |
| 0-99% | **Test** — experiment with list, creative, or offer |
| Negative | **Cut** — unless volume is too low to judge (< 5 deals) |
| No closed deals yet | **Track** — reserve judgment until 50+ leads |

---

## Common Source Analysis Mistakes

1. **Cutting sources too early.** Most sources need 100+ leads and 90+ days to show real conversion data. Don't cut based on 20 leads.
2. **Not tracking labor costs.** Cold calling looks free but costs hours. Include your time at market rate.
3. **Mixing list quality into source analysis.** A bad list gives bad results even with a good channel. Separate list quality from channel performance.
4. **Ignoring deal quality by source.** Some sources produce lower-margin deals. Track average fee by source, not just deal count.
