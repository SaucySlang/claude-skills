# Pipeline Stage Definitions

## Overview

A well-defined pipeline is the difference between a systematic wholesaling business and a chaotic deal-by-deal hustle. Every stage must have clear entry criteria, exit criteria, a responsible owner, and an SLA target.

---

## Stage Definitions

### Stage 1: new_lead
**Definition:** A property/owner combination has been identified as a potential acquisition target but no contact has been attempted.

**Entry criteria:** Lead record created with address, owner name, and source.
**Exit criteria:** First contact attempt logged (call, text, letter sent, door knock).
**SLA target:** 1 business day
**Owner:** Lead manager / acquisitions rep
**Common mistakes:** Letting leads sit while you "research" them. Contact first, research second.

---

### Stage 2: contacted
**Definition:** At least one contact attempt has been made. May or may not have reached the owner.

**Entry criteria:** Contact attempt logged with date, channel, and outcome.
**Exit criteria:** Appointment scheduled (advance) OR lead marked dead (no response after 6 touches over 30 days).
**SLA target:** 7 days to schedule appointment
**Owner:** Acquisitions rep
**Common mistakes:** Marking as "contacted" after one voicemail. Work the lead through your full outreach sequence before moving to dead.

---

### Stage 3: appointment_set
**Definition:** A specific date/time has been confirmed with the seller for an in-person or virtual appointment.

**Entry criteria:** Appointment confirmed verbally or in writing with date/time.
**Exit criteria:** Appointment held (advance) OR appointment canceled/no-show (back to contacted or dead).
**SLA target:** 7 days (appointment should happen within 7 days of being set)
**Owner:** Acquisitions rep
**Common mistakes:** Setting appointments too far out. If a seller says "call me in 6 weeks," that is not an appointment — it is a future contact task.

---

### Stage 4: appointment_complete
**Definition:** The seller meeting has occurred. An offer has not yet been formally submitted.

**Entry criteria:** Appointment held and notes logged.
**Exit criteria:** Offer submitted (advance) OR deal killed at appointment (dead).
**SLA target:** 5 days to submit offer
**Owner:** Acquisitions rep
**Common mistakes:** Leaving appointments without a clear next step. Always leave with either a signed contract, a scheduled offer presentation, or a dead deal.

---

### Stage 5: offer_made
**Definition:** A written offer or LOI has been presented to the seller.

**Entry criteria:** Offer submitted and acknowledged by seller.
**Exit criteria:** Contract signed (advance) OR offer rejected/expired (dead or re-offer).
**SLA target:** 7 days to get a decision
**Owner:** Acquisitions rep / negotiator
**Common mistakes:** Not following up on offers. An offer without follow-up is a wasted appointment.

---

### Stage 6: under_contract
**Definition:** Purchase contract is fully executed by both parties.

**Entry criteria:** Signed contract from both seller and buyer entity.
**Exit criteria:** Deal closes (advance) OR falls through (dead with reason).
**SLA target:** Per contract closing date
**Owner:** Transaction coordinator
**Common mistakes:** Assuming the deal is done. Stay on top of title, buyer funding, and seller questions through the close.

---

### Stage 7: closed
**Definition:** Deed has recorded and assignment fee has been collected.

**Entry criteria:** Deed recording confirmed by title company.
**Exit criteria:** Terminal stage. Log actual fee and lessons learned.
**SLA:** N/A — terminal
**Owner:** Transaction coordinator

---

### Stage 8: dead
**Definition:** Deal has been abandoned or fallen through.

**Entry criteria:** Explicit decision to stop pursuing this deal.
**Exit criteria:** Terminal stage. Always log a dead reason code.
**SLA:** N/A — terminal
**Dead reason codes:** price_gap, seller_not_motivated, title_issue, buyer_backed_out, lost_to_competition, property_condition, seller_relisted, no_response_exhausted
