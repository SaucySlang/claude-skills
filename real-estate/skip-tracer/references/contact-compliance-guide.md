# Contact Compliance Guide

TCPA, DNC registry, and state-specific compliance rules for real estate investor outreach via phone, text, and mail.

---

## TCPA Overview (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)

The TCPA governs how businesses may contact consumers by phone and text. Violations carry statutory damages of $500–$1,500 per call or text.

**Key rules:**

- **Autodialing and pre-recorded calls** require prior express written consent if calling a mobile phone. Human-dialed calls to mobile phones without an autodialer do not require consent under TCPA.
- **Text messages (SMS/MMS)** are treated like calls. Unsolicited marketing texts to mobile numbers require prior written consent.
- **Calling hours:** Calls permitted 8am–9pm in the recipient's local time zone. Calling outside these hours is a TCPA violation.
- **Opt-out:** Any consumer who says "stop calling," "remove me," "don't call again," or similar must be added to your internal DNC list immediately.

**Ringless Voicemail (RVM):** Legal status is contested. Courts are split on whether RVM constitutes a "call" under TCPA. Some investors use RVM; others avoid it pending clearer guidance. Consult counsel before deploying at scale.

---

## National DNC Registry

The FTC's National Do Not Call Registry applies to residential phone numbers. Real estate investors conducting marketing calls must comply.

**Requirements:**
- Access the registry at donotcall.gov (subscription required for commercial use)
- Scrub your call list against the registry before each campaign
- Do not call numbers that have been on the registry for 31+ days
- Internal DNC list must also be maintained and applied in addition to the national registry

**B2B exemption:** Calls to business numbers are generally exempt from the national DNC registry. Property owner calls are considered B2C even if the owner is an investor.

## State-Specific Rules

Several states have stricter rules than federal TCPA:

| State | Key Rule |
|---|---|
| California | CCPA data rights; California DNC list separate from federal |
| Florida | Telemarketing act requires registration for commercial callers |
| Texas | Must honor DNC requests within 10 days; separate state registry |
| New York | Strict autodial consent rules; aggressive AG enforcement |
| Indiana | State DNC list; cross-reference required |

Always check the state-specific rules for any market you are calling into.

---

## Consent Documentation

If you collect written consent (e.g., through a landing page or lead form), document:

- Date and time of consent
- IP address or device identifier
- Exact language of the consent disclosure shown
- Method of consent (checkbox, signature, etc.)

Store consent records for a minimum of 4 years (the TCPA statute of limitations).

---

## Internal DNC List Best Practices

1. Add any opt-out request within 24 hours — ideally immediately
2. Store opt-outs with date, channel of request, and exact language used
3. Apply internal DNC list on every outreach run, before the national registry scrub
4. Share the internal DNC list across all campaigns — a number opted out of one campaign is opted out of all
5. Train all callers and virtual assistants on immediate DNC logging

---

## Mail Compliance

Direct mail to property owners is not subject to TCPA or DNC. However:

- Honor written cease-and-desist letters immediately
- Do not use deceptive envelope language suggesting government correspondence
- Include your return address and business identity on all mail pieces
- USPS regulations prohibit false or misleading representations
