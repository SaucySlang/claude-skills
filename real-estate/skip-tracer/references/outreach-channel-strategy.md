# Outreach Channel Strategy

When to call, text, mail, or email property owners — with response rate benchmarks, timing best practices, and voicemail guidance.

---

## Channel Response Rate Benchmarks

These benchmarks are based on real estate investor outreach to motivated seller lists (absentee owners, pre-foreclosure, probate). Results vary by list quality and market.

| Channel | Response Rate | Best Use Case | Cost per Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold call (human) | 3–8% | Verified mobile, high match confidence | $0.10–$0.30 |
| Text / SMS | 5–15% | Verified mobile, first contact | $0.01–$0.05 |
| Direct mail (letter) | 1–4% | All leads including DNC | $0.50–$1.50 |
| Email | 1–3% | Leads with verified email | $0.001–$0.01 |
| Ringless voicemail | 2–6% | Medium-confidence lists | $0.05–$0.15 |

**Key insight:** Text has the highest response rate per dollar spent. Direct mail has the lowest response rate but reaches leads that cannot be contacted any other way.

## When to Use Each Channel

### Call First
- Contact score 70+ (Grade A)
- Mobile phone verified
- Owner-occupied or absentee owner with high match confidence
- Probate or pre-foreclosure (time-sensitive motivation)

### Text First
- Contact score 40–69 (Grade B)
- Mobile confirmed but not phone_verified
- Owner is younger (under 55 demographic)
- Market is competitive and speed matters

### Email First
- Verified email, no phone or phone not verified
- Lead came from an online source (more comfortable with digital)
- Prior mail sent with no response

### Mail Only
- DNC flagged
- No phone or email data returned
- Trust/entity owner (hard to reach by phone)
- Very low match confidence (do not risk calling wrong person)

## Calling Best Practices

**Best times to call (local time of recipient):**
- Tuesday–Thursday: 10am–12pm and 4pm–6pm
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
- Never call before 8am or after 9pm (TCPA requirement)

**Call frequency:** Do not call the same number more than 2–3 times per week. Excessive calls increase complaint risk and damage answer rates over time.

**Caller ID:** Use a local area code number. Local numbers have 40–60% higher answer rates than toll-free or out-of-state numbers.

**Script discipline:** Open with your name, who you are, and a single clear question. Do not pitch before establishing interest. The goal of the first call is to get them talking, not to close a deal.

## Voicemail Best Practices

- Keep voicemails under 30 seconds
- State your name and phone number twice (once at beginning, once at end)
- Mention the property address — it proves you know their specific property
- Do not mention price on the first voicemail
- Vary your voicemail script across touches — identical messages trigger spam filters on visual voicemail apps

---

## Text Message Best Practices

- Always include an opt-out instruction ("Reply STOP to opt out")
- Send between 9am–7pm local time
- Keep first text under 160 characters when possible
- Personalize with owner name and property address
- Do not send more than 2–3 texts before calling
- Use a dedicated texting platform (e.g., Launch Control, REISift, FollowUpBoss) — not a personal cell phone at scale

---

## Direct Mail Best Practices

- Yellow letters (handwritten-style) outperform printed corporate letters for motivated sellers
- Use a return address in the same city as the property
- Mail to the mailing address, not the property address (absentee owners may not check the property mailbox)
- 6x9 postcards work well for follow-up touches; letters work better for initial contact
- Track responses with a dedicated phone number or URL per campaign
