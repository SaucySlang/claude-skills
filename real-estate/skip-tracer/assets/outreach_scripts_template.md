# Outreach Scripts Template

Use these scripts for initial contact and follow-up touches across all channels. Customize the bracketed fields for each lead before use.

---

## Phone Scripts

### Initial Call — `initial_call`

> "Hi, is this [OWNER_NAME]? Great. My name is [YOUR_NAME] and I'm a local real estate investor here in [CITY]. I actually came across your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS] and wanted to reach out directly to see if you'd ever considered selling. I'm not a real estate agent — I buy directly and can close quickly without hassle. Do you have two minutes?"

**If they say yes:** Move to motivation discovery. Ask: "What would make selling the right move for you right now?"

**If no answer:** Leave voicemail (see voicemail script below).

---

### Second Call — `second_call`

> "Hi [OWNER_NAME], this is [YOUR_NAME] again. I reached out last week about your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS]. I'm still interested in making a fair cash offer and wanted to follow up before I move on. If you have a few minutes I'd love to chat. You can call me back at [YOUR_PHONE]. No pressure at all — thanks."

---

### Voicemail Script

> "Hi, this is a message for [OWNER_NAME]. My name is [YOUR_NAME] and I'm a local real estate buyer. I was hoping to talk with you about your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS]. I can often close in as little as [X] days and there are no agent fees or repairs needed. Please call me back at [YOUR_PHONE] when you get a chance. Again, that's [YOUR_PHONE]. Thank you."

**Keep voicemails under 30 seconds. State your name and number twice.**

---

## Text Message Scripts

### Initial Text — `initial_text`

> Hi [OWNER_NAME], this is [YOUR_NAME], a local real estate buyer. I'm interested in your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS]. Would you be open to a fair cash offer? No agents or fees. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Character limit: 160. Always include opt-out language.**

### Follow-Up Text — `follow_up_text`

> Hi [OWNER_NAME], following up on my message from [DATE]. Still interested in [PROPERTY_ADDRESS] if you'd ever consider selling. No pressure — reply STOP to opt out or call me at [YOUR_PHONE].

## Email Scripts

### Email Intro — `email_intro`

**Subject:** Your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS] — quick question

> Hi [OWNER_NAME],
>
> My name is [YOUR_NAME] and I'm a real estate investor based in [CITY]. I came across your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS] and wanted to reach out directly.
>
> If you've ever considered selling — even just exploring your options — I'd love to have a quick conversation. I buy properties directly from owners, which means:
>
> - No agent commissions or fees
> - No repairs or cleanup required
> - Flexible closing timeline (as fast as [X] days or as long as you need)
>
> If you're interested, just reply to this email or call me at [YOUR_PHONE]. If you're not interested, no worries at all — I appreciate your time.
>
> Best,
> [YOUR_NAME]
> [YOUR_COMPANY]
> [YOUR_PHONE]

## Direct Mail Scripts

### Direct Mail Letter — `direct_mail_letter`

> Dear [OWNER_NAME],
>
> My name is [YOUR_NAME] and I am a local real estate investor. I am writing to you directly because I am interested in purchasing your property at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS].
>
> I buy properties as-is — no repairs, no clean-up, no agent fees. If you have been thinking about selling or simply want to explore your options, I would be happy to make you a fair cash offer with no obligation.
>
> Please call or text me at [YOUR_PHONE] or email [YOUR_EMAIL]. I look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Sincerely,
> [YOUR_NAME]
> [YOUR_COMPANY]

>
