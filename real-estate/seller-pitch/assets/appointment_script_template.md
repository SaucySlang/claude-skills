# Seller Appointment Script Template

## Pre-Arrival (In the Car)

Review your appointment prep brief. Know these three numbers cold:
- ARV: $_______
- Opening offer: $_______
- Walk-away (MAO): $_______

Say out loud: "I am here to solve a problem, not to buy a house."

---

## Intro (First 2 Minutes)

Knock, introduce yourself warmly.

> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name]. Thanks for having me — I've been looking forward to meeting you. Before we sit down, would you mind if I took a quick walk through the property? It helps me understand what we're working with."

**Do the walkthrough first. Always.**

Walk every room. Take photos with permission. Note condition issues without reacting negatively.

---

## Discovery (10-15 Minutes)

Sit at the kitchen table — never a formal living room. Kitchens signal honesty and conversation.

**Opening question (choose one from your brief):**

> "Before I share what I've found on your property, I'd love to hear your story. What's prompting you to think about selling now?"

**Listen for 80% of this phase. Your job is to understand:**
- What is their real pain? (Timeline, money, property burden, life event)
- What does a "win" look like for them?
- Who else is involved in the decision?

**Key follow-up probes:**
- "Tell me more about that."
- "What happens if this doesn't sell in the next 60 days?"
- "Who else needs to be part of this decision?"
- "What have other investors offered you so far?"

---

## Value Framing (5 Minutes)

Before presenting numbers, establish what you do differently.

> "Before I share my offer, let me explain how we're different from traditional buyers. We buy as-is — no repairs, no inspection contingencies, no financing delays. Our closes happen in [X] days. You pick the date that works for you. There are no commissions, no closing costs on your side. What you see is what you net."

---

## Comp Presentation (5 Minutes)

Pull up comps on your tablet. Show 3 comps.

> "I pulled the three most recent sales within a mile of your home. They closed at $___, $___, and $___, which puts your home's retail value around $[ARV]. Now, that's the retail number — what a buyer would pay after it's been fixed up and listed. We're working with the as-is value, which accounts for the repairs needed."

Key point: Let them do the math with you. Do not just announce a number.

---

## Offer Delivery

> "Based on everything I've seen — the condition, the comps, and the timeline you're working with — I can offer you $[opening offer]. That's cash, as-is, and we can close on [specific date they mentioned]. How does that feel?"

**Then stop talking.** The next person to speak loses the frame.

If they counter, you have room between your opening offer and MAO.

If they say "I need more," respond:
> "I hear you. Help me understand — if I could get to $[counter], does that solve the problem for you? Because I want to make this work."

---

## Handling Objections

Use the FEEL-FELT-FOUND framework for all objections. See `objection_handler_selector.py` for full scripts.

**The three rules:**
1. Never argue about price — reframe to net proceeds
2. Never push past three "no" responses — it means the deal is dead today
3. Always leave with a defined next step

---

## Closing (2 Paths)

**Path A — Signs today:**
> "Excellent. Let me pull up the purchase agreement. This is a standard contract — let me walk you through the key terms."

Explain: Purchase price, closing date, as-is clause, earnest money, title company used.

**Path B — Does not sign today:**
> "That's completely fine — I respect you wanting to think it through. Can I ask — is there anything specific holding you back right now? [Listen.] Okay. Can we set a time to talk Thursday at 10am? I'll have my answers ready for any questions that come up."

Leave your business card plus a one-page deal summary with the net sheet.

---

## Post-Appointment (Within 24 Hours)

- Log outcome in CRM with full notes
- Send handwritten thank-you card (warm and unconverted leads only)
- Set follow-up task: Day 3, Day 7, Day 21, Day 60
- If deal is dead, log reason and remove from active pipeline
