# Buyer Acquisition Strategies

## Why Buyer List Quality Determines Wholesale Profitability

In wholesaling, you make money at assignment — and you can only assign if a buyer closes. The speed at which you can move a deal from contract to buyer assignment directly determines your holding cost, deal velocity, and reputation with sellers. A shallow buyer list creates bottlenecks; a deep, segmented list creates leverage.

The goal is not the largest possible list. It is the most verified, active, and responsive list — buyers who have proven they close, respond quickly, and have clear, consistent criteria.

## High-Signal Acquisition Sources

### 1. Courthouse Auction Lists

Tax deed sales, foreclosure auctions, and sheriff's sales attract verified cash buyers — these individuals have already committed to buying real estate without financing. Attend monthly auctions in your target county and collect names of every winning bidder. After the auction, introduce yourself directly: "I wholesale off-market deals in this area — are you looking for more inventory?" These are among the warmest buyer conversations you can have.

Courthouse auction lists are also sometimes available from county clerks as public record. Request the past 12 months of winning bidder names and match against county recorder data to build a targeted outreach list.

### 2. Title Company Referrals

Title companies see every cash closing in your market. A single title rep who handles 20-30 investor closings per month has direct visibility into your most active buyers. Build relationships with 2-3 title reps who specialize in investor transactions. Offer to bring them deals (which gives them closing fees) in exchange for introductions to cash buyers. This is one of the most reliable long-term referral channels in wholesaling.

Ask specifically: "Who are your top 5 cash buyers this quarter?" A good rep will make the introduction.

### 3. Real Estate Investor Meetups and REIA Groups

Local Real Estate Investor Association (REIA) chapters hold monthly meetings attended by active buyers. Do not attend to pitch deals — attend to build relationships. Introduce yourself as a wholesaler with consistent deal flow, hand out cards, and follow up within 48 hours. Many cities have multiple active investor groups; prioritize the ones with highest membership and most active deal discussion.

Facebook groups are the online equivalent. Join every local "real estate investors [city]" group. Post once introducing yourself as a local wholesaler with off-market inventory. Reply to every "looking for deals" post. Message individual buyers who post deal purchase announcements.

### 4. County Recorder Cash Deed Search

The county recorder's office records every property deed. Deeds with no corresponding mortgage recorded — or deeds showing "cash" as consideration — identify buyers who paid cash at closing. These are your most reliable buyer signals.

Search the past 6-12 months of residential deed transfers. Filter for properties where no mortgage was recorded within 30 days of the deed. Cross-reference buyer names against public contact databases for phone and email. This is time-intensive but produces the highest-quality leads.

### 5. "Bandit Sign" Reverse Campaigns

Instead of driving for deals, post signs near known flip projects: "Wholesale deals available — cash buyers call [number]." Investors actively rehabbing in a neighborhood are by definition cash buyers looking for more inventory. These calls are highly qualified and self-selecting.

Position signs at the perimeter of active rehab projects, near investor-heavy zip codes, and at local hardware stores where contractors shop.

### 6. Hard Money Lender Networks

Hard money lenders fund the deals your buyers need capital for. Lenders who decline deals due to borrower qualifications — but trust the deal itself — will sometimes refer those borrowers to wholesalers who can restructure. Contact local hard money lenders and offer a referral relationship for buyers who can't get funded but who have cash for wholesale assignments.

## Acquisition Rate Benchmarks

| Source | Effort Level | Typical Quality |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Courthouse auctions | Medium | Very high |
| Title company referrals | Low | Very high |
| REIA meetups | Medium | High |
| Recorder search | High | High |
| Facebook groups | Low | Medium |
| Bandit signs | Low | Medium-high |

Target 5-10 new buyer conversations per week when building a list from scratch. Quality screen every new contact before adding to active segments.
