## Intake Checklist

Before marking a buyer as "active" in the system:

- [ ] All Section 1-4 fields completed
- [ ] Proof of funds letter received and verified
- [ ] At least one verified closed deal confirmed
- [ ] Buyer added to correct segment (type + grade) in CRM
- [ ] Buyer added to market-specific blast list
- [ ] Welcome text or email sent confirming they are on the list
