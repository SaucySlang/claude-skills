### 6. Assignor's Representations and Warranties

Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee that:

a) The Original Agreement is in full force and effect and has not been modified, canceled, or terminated;

b) Assignor has not assigned, pledged, or otherwise transferred any interest in the Original Agreement to any other person or entity;

c) Assignor is not in default under the Original Agreement;

d) The Original Agreement permits assignment without the seller's consent, OR written consent of the seller has been obtained (attached hereto as Exhibit B).

### 7. As-Is Purchase

Assignee acknowledges that Assignee is purchasing the Property in its "AS-IS, WHERE-IS" condition. Assignee has had the opportunity to conduct its own due diligence, inspections, and investigations. Assignor makes no representations or warranties regarding the condition of the Property.

### 8. Indemnification

Assignee agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Assignor from and against any claims, losses, damages, costs, and expenses (including attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to Assignee's performance or non-performance of obligations under the Original Agreement from and after the date of this Assignment.

### 9. Non-Circumvention

Assignee agrees not to contact the Seller directly regarding the purchase of the Property except through channels coordinated by Assignor or the title company. Assignee shall not attempt to renegotiate the purchase terms with the Seller without Assignor's written consent.

### 10. Default

If Assignee fails to close on the Property as required by the Original Agreement, Assignee shall forfeit the non-refundable deposit and shall be liable to Assignor for any damages, including loss of the Assignment Fee balance, incurred as a result of such failure.

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of _______________. Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall be resolved in _______________ County, State of _______________.

### 12. Entire Agreement

This Assignment of Purchase and Sale Agreement, together with the Original Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
